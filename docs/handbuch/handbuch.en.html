lwhub – Manual

lwhub is a web hub for Last War alliances. Every week, R4/R5 record who showed up for Guard, Desert Storm, the special event, VS and donations. That turns into points every member can check for themselves. On top of that you get a profession planner, a train planner and a screenshot scanner that reads right in your browser. This manual covers each feature: what it's for, who can see it and how to use it.

Many areas are feature switches: your alliance decides what it uses. Anything switched off disappears from the menu. The full list is under Settings.

👥 Who sees what?

lwhub follows your in-game rank. The server only sends the R4/R5 pages and the admin area to members with the right rank.

🤝 Bringing your alliance on board

What's it for?

lwhub is one platform shared by several alliances. Each alliance has its own tag and only ever sees its own data.

How it works

  1. Get in touch: reach out to Lion Tooth on server 776 in game. Lion Tooth creates your alliance with tag and name and turns on "accepts login requests".
  2. Log in with Discord and send a request: open lwhub and log in with Discord. If your Discord isn't linked to a member yet, you'll see "No access? Send a request". Pick your alliance, enter your player name, send.
  3. Get approved and start: the operator adds your first leader (R5). From then on, R4/R5 handle new requests themselves under Management, turn on the features they want and add members – by hand or with the scanner.

Tips & limits

  • An alliance only shows up in the list if it accepts login requests.
  • A request doesn't create a member yet. An R4/R5 decides: add as new member, link to an existing member, or reject.

🔑 Discord login & switching characters

Who sees it?Everyone
Feature switch?No, always on

What's it for?

No extra passwords. You log in with your Discord account. lwhub only asks Discord for the basics (the identify scope: ID, name, avatar) – no servers, no messages.

How it works

  1. Tap "Log in with Discord" and confirm on Discord.
  2. lwhub looks for the active member with your Discord ID. Found: you're in. Next time it logs you in automatically as long as your Discord login is still valid.
  3. Not found: you can send a login request to your alliance.

Several characters

Plenty of players run more than one account, sometimes in different alliances. Every character with the same Discord ID is tied to your login. Use the character switcher to pick the active one, and lwhub shows that character's alliance and data. You can also rename your character there ("Rename character"); the name history is kept.

Tips & limits

  • Logging out removes your login and selected character from the browser.
  • Rank and rights are per character. Being R4 in alliance A doesn't make you R4 in alliance B.

🎉 Events

Who sees it?R4/R5 (honorary R4 and operator read-only)
Feature switch?Yes: main switch events plus one per event: wache, wueste, spezial, vs, spende

What's it for?

This is where you record, week by week, who took part in which event. Each member is a tile; tap it to set the status. All the ticks of the week turn into the points members see in My Area.

How it works

  1. Open "Events" and pick the calendar week with ‹ ›.
  2. Choose the event at the top: Guard 1–3, Desert, Special, VS or Donations.
  3. Tap tiles to set the status. It saves right away.
  4. "↓ Done to the end" moves finished members down (click again to hide them). "◐ High contrast" helps on a phone in bright light.

🛡 Guard 1–3

Three Guard runs a week, one tab each. Per member: good damage ⚔, low damage ⚠ (under 2G) or absent ○. Recorded by hand – there's no scanner import.

🏜 Desert Storm

Three states: signed up 📋, took part ⚔, and unexcused absence ⚠ (penalty). Recorded by hand.

🎯 Special event

Participation ⚔, Top 50 🏅, Top 20 🏆. Which event runs this week is set for the whole server – see special event per server.

Import from scanner: sets the ticks from the before/after event analysis. Anyone with kills or a power loss counts as present. By kills: places 1–50 get Top 50, places 1–20 get Top 20. The magnifier 🔍 opens the analysis in its own window.

⚔ VS with low-score week

Top 10 🏆 and Top 30 🥈 earn a bonus; anyone below the weekly threshold (currently 43.2M) gets ⚠.

Low-score week 🐷: some weeks your alliance deliberately keeps VS points low. Then: at most 10M per VS day (target 7.2M) earns the bonus, anything above gets no bonus, and there's no penalty. The "Low-score week" button sets or clears this for the selected week.

Import from scanner: uses the scanned weekly VS points. Places 1–10 Top 10, 1–30 Top 30, below the threshold penalty.

💰 Donations

Top 20 🏅 earns a bonus, under 35K ⚠ is a penalty.

Import from scanner: uses the latest donation scan of the selected week (its timestamp is shown next to the button). Places 1–20 Top 20, under 35K penalty.

Tips & limits

  • The scanner import only sets ticks. You can fix any tile by hand afterwards.
  • The thresholds (43.2M, 35K, 2G) are shown in the legend above the tiles.

🗓 Special event per server and week

Who sees it?R4/R5 under Events → Special
RequiresFeature spezial on and a server number set

What's it for?

The special event changes every week and applies to the whole server. Alliances with the same server number therefore share the schedule: one enters it, everyone else sees it.

How it works

  1. Pick the week and type the name in "Which event?" (earlier names are suggested).
  2. Enter the day, start and end (default 16:00–20:00) and hit "💾 Set". This applies to every alliance on the server.
  3. "🚫 No event": there's no special event on the server this week.
  4. "⏸ Don't evaluate": only your alliance skips this week's event. Other alliances aren't affected.

Tips & limits

  • The event analysis uses the day and time to find the before and after scans.
  • Under Management, "No special event in recent weeks" lists everyone who missed it for 2–6 weeks.

⚗ Profession planner & rank check

Who sees it?Planner: R4/R5. Own profession: every member in My Area.
Feature switch?Yes (berufe). Rank check: setting in the planner (R4/R5).
Two ways to assign ranks. Most alliances now give out R2 and R3 by profession – that's what this section and the rank check are for. If you prefer ranks by points, that's a separate option under Optional: rank by points. The two don't mix: once professions decide the rank, leave the points advice off.

What's it for?

Warlords and Engineers go together: every Warlord gets their Engineers. The planner shows this as columns you drag members into. The number of Engineer slots per Warlord is adjustable (1–10, default 3).

How it works

  1. On the left is the queue: members without an assignment.
  2. Drag them into the Warlord column or into an Engineer slot.
  3. Sort by name or by number of Engineers.
  4. "✉ Alliance mail" copies the finished line-up as text, ready to paste into an in-game mail.

Rank shields and rank check

Each tile shows the rank as a number in a shield in the rank's colour. Many alliances assign R2 and R3 by profession, because the game doesn't show professions in the member list. That's where the R2/R3 rank check comes in: switch it on and choose whether R2 means Engineer or Warlord. Tiles are tinted by the expected profession. Anyone in the wrong spot gets a yellow border, wiggles and leans towards the right column.

🎚 Optional: rank by points

Only for alliances that assign R1–R3 by points. If your ranks follow professions (R2/R3 = Engineer/Warlord), you don't need this. Leave the switch off and use the rank check.
Who sees it?R4/R5 ("Results" tab)
Feature switch?Yes (ergebnis), default: off

What's it for?

If your alliance assigns ranks by points, lwhub suggests who has earned a higher rank. The rule: up to 84 points R1, from 85 R2, from 180 R3. R4 and R5 stay fixed.

Example · 4 weeks
KiwiR2190205188210Promote → R3

How it works

  1. Pick a period (3–6 weeks) and hit "Load".
  2. Green: promotion suggested (stable for at least 2 weeks). Red: the rank doesn't match the points right now (for more than 2 weeks) – a good reason for a friendly chat. Yellow: AFK reported, the rank stays as it is. Grey: no data yet.
  3. Change the rank in game first, then tap the button. lwhub asks you to confirm it's done in game and then updates the rank.

🗂 Member management

Who sees it?R4/R5 (honorary R4 read-only). Admin area: R5 and operator.
Feature switch?No, always on

What's it for?

Keeping the member list in shape: new players, rank changes, Discord links, guests from other alliances, departures.

How it works

  1. Add member: enter name, Discord ID (optional) and rank.
  2. Login requests: accept open requests "as new member", "map to member" (link to an existing member), or reject them.
  3. Member list: tap a member to change name, Discord ID, rank and role. You also see when they last logged in.
  4. Removing: deleted players go to the archive. R5 can restore them with all their data from Admin → Archive.

Honorary roles

👁 Honorary R4

A guest from another alliance, e.g. a friendly R4. Sees everything an R4 sees, changes nothing and is never counted. Enter their home alliance and Discord ID so they can log in.

🎖 Honorary member

Stays on the list without write access. The rank check skips honorary members.

Admin area (R5)

  • Alliances: change tag and name. Renaming the tag moves all player data along. Per alliance: "accepts login requests" and the feature switches.
  • Archive: restore deleted players.
  • Transfer: move a player with all their data to another alliance.
  • Merge: combine duplicate entries (e.g. after a name change) into one player.
  • API tokens: see Android app.

📸 Screenshot scanner

Who sees it?R4/R5 without an honorary role. Honorary R4 and operator only see the power trend.
Feature switch?Yes (scanner)

What's it for?

Instead of typing 100 names: just scroll through the member list, power ranking, VS points (daily and weekly), kills and donations and let the scanner read them. Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely in your browser. Images are never uploaded – only the recognised values go to lwhub.

How it works

  1. Open the "Scanner" tab. On a PC, "Open in its own window" is faster.
  2. Either "Choose window & start scan", share the game window (e.g. an emulator) and scroll slowly – changed views are captured automatically. Or "Load screenshots / video".
  3. On the left is your lwhub member list, in the middle the pool of recognised tiles. Clear matches are assigned automatically.
  4. Drag unclear tiles onto the right member, or onto "New player" to add a newcomer. Anyone no longer in the alliance ends up under "Archive".
  5. Sync. The scanner then shows what was imported and where there were conflicts.

Tips & limits

  • Processing: graphics card (WebGPU) by default, otherwise CPU. You can set how many CPU cores to use.
  • The scanner flags gaps in the rank numbers (e.g. if you scrolled too fast).
  • You can download your captures locally for later, or discard them.
  • Players from other alliances in a list are recognised by their alliance tag and left out.

📈 Power trend

Who sees it?R4/R5, honorary R4, operator
Feature switch?Part of scanner

What's it for?

How is your alliance's power developing? Active players grow during the week and put their troops to work in the big Saturday event. The trend makes that rhythm visible from your power scans, so leadership can see who's really taking off and who might appreciate a tip or a helping hand.

Verdicts
🔥 sacrifices on Saturday📈 growing➖ normal⚠️ stagnant❔ not enough data

How it works

  1. Scan the power ranking regularly. Best: Friday (before the event), Sunday (after) and once mid-week.
  2. Open the trend, filter or sort by verdict.
  3. If needed, "Adjust thresholds", e.g. what percentage of Saturday loss counts.

🔍 Event analysis (before/after)

Who sees it?R4/R5 with scan rights
Feature switch?Part of scanner

What's it for?

"Who showed up for the event?" – answered from two scans, one before and one after. Anyone who got kills or lost power was there.

How it works

  1. Scan power and kills once before and once after the event.
  2. Open the analysis (Events → Special, via 🔍). Set day, start, end, grace period in minutes, "kills from" and "loss from (%)".
  3. Check the result, use "📋 Copy names" for chat, or set the ticks in the Events tab with "Import from scanner".

📱 Android app & API tokens

Who sees it?Managing tokens: R5 and operator (Admin → API tokens)
StatusThe app is in testing

What's it for?

An Android app reads rankings right on your phone (e.g. power, VS, donations) and uploads the values to lwhub. So that tools like the app can write without a Discord login, there are API tokens.

How it works

  1. Admin → API tokens → choose the alliance → "+ Create token".
  2. Give it a name (what is it for?) and pick a role: Scanner (values for known players only), R4 or R5 (same as in the web scanner). Expiry is optional; empty means no expiry.
  3. Copy the token right away. It's only shown once.
  4. Not needed any more? "Revoke token". The list shows when each token was last used.

Tips & limits

  • Risky actions such as archiving, transfers or rank changes are not possible with an app token.
  • Lion Tooth (server 776) can tell you whether and how your alliance can get the app.

🔒 Privacy

This is a factual overview of what lwhub stores, based on its code. It is not legal advice and not a privacy policy.

On the server

  • Discord: your Discord ID, name and avatar (cached). Only the identify scope is requested.
  • Player: player name and previous names, alliance, rank and rank changes, role, profession and preferred language.
  • Weekly data: event ticks, positive and penalty points per calendar week.
  • Teams: team types, power and hero power, if you enter them.
  • Scan values: recognised power, VS points, kills and donations with a timestamp. The screenshots themselves are not uploaded.
  • Train: schedule, queue, no-shows.
  • Presence: while lwhub is open, "last seen" is saved about every 5 minutes. R4/R5 can see the last login.
  • Login requests: Discord name, avatar, chosen alliance, player name and your note.
  • Archive: deleted players are archived with their data so R5 can restore them.
  • Security: checked or rejected API calls are logged with time, path, Discord ID and IP address. Discord logins are cached for 15 minutes, as a hash only. API tokens are stored as a hash only.

In your browser

Your Discord login, active character, language and last opened page are kept in your browser's local storage. "Log out" removes the login and character.

Who can see the data?

Each alliance only sees its own members. Within the alliance, the roles from Who sees what? apply. Special event schedules are shared between alliances with the same server number.
