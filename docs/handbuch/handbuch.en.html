lwhub – Manual

lwhub is a web hub for Last War alliances. Every week, R4/R5 record who showed up for Guard, Desert Storm, the special event, VS and donations. That turns into points every member can check for themselves. On top of that you get a profession planner, a train planner and a screenshot scanner that reads right in your browser. This manual covers each feature: what it's for, who can see it and how to use it.

Many areas are feature switches: your alliance decides what it uses. Anything switched off disappears from the menu. The full list is under Settings.

👥 Who sees what?

lwhub follows your in-game rank. The server only sends the R4/R5 pages and the admin area to members with the right rank.

🤝 Bringing your alliance on board

What's it for?

lwhub is one platform shared by several alliances. Each alliance has its own tag and only ever sees its own data.

How it works

  1. Get in touch: reach out to Lion Tooth on server 776 in game. Lion Tooth creates your alliance with tag and name and turns on "accepts login requests".
  2. Log in with Discord and send a request: open lwhub and log in with Discord. If your Discord isn't linked to a member yet, you'll see "No access? Send a request". Pick your alliance, enter your player name, send.
  3. Get approved and start: the operator adds your first leader (R5). From then on, R4/R5 handle new requests themselves under Management, turn on the features they want and add members – by hand or with the scanner.

Tips & limits

  • An alliance only shows up in the list if it accepts login requests.
  • A request doesn't create a member yet. An R4/R5 decides: add as new member, link to an existing member, or reject.

🔑 Discord login & switching characters

Who sees it?Everyone
Feature switch?No, always on

What's it for?

No extra passwords. You log in with your Discord account. lwhub only asks Discord for the basics (the identify scope: ID, name, avatar) – no servers, no messages.

How it works

  1. Tap "Log in with Discord" and confirm on Discord.
  2. lwhub looks for the active member with your Discord ID. Found: you're in. Next time it logs you in automatically as long as your Discord login is still valid.
  3. Not found: you can send a login request to your alliance.

Several characters

Plenty of players run more than one account, sometimes in different alliances. Every character with the same Discord ID is tied to your login. Use the character switcher to pick the active one, and lwhub shows that character's alliance and data. You can also rename your character there ("Rename character"); the name history is kept.

Tips & limits

  • Logging out removes your login and selected character from the browser.
  • Rank and rights are per character. Being R4 in alliance A doesn't make you R4 in alliance B.

How it works

  1. Open "Events" and pick the calendar week with ‹ ›.
  2. Choose the event at the top: Guard 1–3, Desert, Special, VS or Donations. The first category with ticks opens by itself; whatever you pick yourself stays.

🛡 Guard 1–3

Three Guard runs a week, one tab each. Per member: good damage ⚔, low damage ⚠ (under 2G) or absent ○. Recorded by hand – there's no scanner import.

🏜 Desert Storm

Three states: signed up 📋, took part ⚔, and unexcused absence ⚠ (penalty). You can set them by hand – or let the scanner do it for you.

With the scanner (R4/R5): The scanner compares who was assigned to team A and B with who actually fought. Team B fights at midday, team A in the evening. The scanner page is in German, so the button names below are given in German.

  1. In the game, open the mail “[Wüstensturm]-Kampfergebnis!” (Desert Storm battle result) and scroll slowly through the points list to the very bottom. Do this for both mails (midday and evening).
  2. Open Events → Desert Storm → Logs and scroll back to Monday of that week. This tells the scanner who was assigned as participant or substitute.
  3. The box “🏜 Wüstensturm” appears above the tile pool: per team, who signed up and did not show up.
  4. If it says “Nicht erfasst: Platz 13” (place 13 not captured), a row was skipped while scrolling. Just scroll slowly over that part of the mail again.
  5. If someone writes their name in mirror writing (backwards, e.g. “oɔɔoᗡ lƎ”), the scanner reads the row again mirrored. The log then says “🪞 Spiegelschrift gelesen” (mirror writing read).
  6. If a name still can't be read (e.g. heavily decorated), the box shows the row as a picture. Pick the player under “Wer ist das?” (who is this?).
  7. “📋 Ergebnis kopieren” copies the text for Discord or a mail. “🚀 Häkchen nach LWHub” sets the ticks in the Desert tab for that week. A confirmation first shows who gets “absent”.

🎯 Special event

Participation ⚔, Top 50 🏅, Top 20 🏆. Which event runs this week is set for the whole server – see special event per server.

Import from scanner: sets the ticks from the before/after event analysis. Anyone with kills or a power loss counts as present. By kills: places 1–50 get Top 50, places 1–20 get Top 20. The magnifier 🔍 takes you to the analysis (Reports → Server events).

⚔ VS with low-score week

Top 10 🏆 and Top 30 🥈 earn a bonus; anyone below the weekly threshold (currently 43.2M) gets ⚠.

Low-score week 🐷: some weeks your alliance deliberately keeps VS points low. Then: at most 10M per VS day (target 7.2M) earns the bonus, anything above gets no bonus, and there's no penalty. The "Low-score week" button sets or clears this for the selected week.

Automatic when scanning: as soon as VS values are uploaded, lwhub sets the week's ticks itself: places 1–10 Top 10, 1–30 Top 30. Top places shift with every scan. The penalty only comes once the weekly list has been scanned on Saturday – mid-week almost everyone is still below it. Anyone skipped while scrolling the weekly list gets their place from the sum of their days (top places only, never a penalty).

Import from scanner runs the same calculation by hand, with a preview.

💰 Donations

Top 20 🏅 earns a bonus, under 35K ⚠ is a penalty.

Automatic when scanning: like VS – places 1–20 Top 20 right away, "under 35K" only with Saturday's scan of the weekly list. Scanning until Saturday evening is enough. "Import from scanner" runs it by hand (the scan time is shown next to the button).

Tips & limits

  • Every scanned player counts as recorded, even without ticks – the solid middle gets its base points and no longer shows as "pending".
  • You can fix any tile by hand. If you correct a player in VS or Donations, the next scan leaves them alone for that week. "Import from scanner" releases them again.
  • The thresholds (43.2M, 35K, 2G) are shown in the legend above the tiles.

🗓 Special event per server and week

Who sees it?R4/R5 under Events → Special
RequiresFeature spezial on and a server number set

What's it for?

The special event changes every week and applies to the whole server. Alliances with the same server number therefore share the schedule: one enters it, everyone else sees it.

How it works

  1. Pick the week and type the name in "Which event?" (earlier names are suggested).
  2. Enter the day, start and end and hit "💾 Set". This applies to every alliance on the server. For a new week lwhub suggests the time of the last event.
  3. "🚫 No event": there's no special event on the server this week.
  4. "⏸ Don't evaluate": only your alliance skips this week's event. Other alliances aren't affected.

Tips & limits

  • In the game the event runs at a fixed server time (UTC−2). For us in Germany that's e.g. 16:00 in summer and 15:00 in winter. lwhub converts the suggestion via server time and shows it next to the fields.
  • The event analysis uses the day and time to find the before and after scans.
  • Under Management, "No special event in recent weeks" lists everyone who missed it for 2–6 weeks.

📊 Reports & dashboard

Who sees it?R4/R5 (trend and server events also honorary R4 and operator)
Feature switch?No, always on

The "Reports" tab puts everything you look at in one place. Pick the view at the top:

  • 📋 Dashboard: the weekly overview for leadership – how many members are already recorded for the selected week, warnings, top performers, rank distribution, the most common entries and a filterable table. Older weeks count the members of that week.
  • 🏅 Points overview: points per player over several weeks, with a total. Click a column to sort, click again to reverse.
  • 📈 Power trend and ⚔ Server events (the event analysis) – right here, no detour via the scanner.

Risers, fallers, the "Trend" column and the rank advice in the points overview only appear with rank by points. If your alliance assigns ranks by profession, they stay hidden.

⚗ Profession planner & rank check

Who sees it?Planner: R4/R5. Own profession: every member in My Area.
Feature switch?Yes (berufe). Rank check: setting in the planner (R4/R5).
Two ways to assign ranks. Most alliances now give out R2 and R3 by profession – that's what this section and the rank check are for. If you prefer ranks by points, that's a separate option under Optional: rank by points. The two don't mix: once professions decide the rank, leave the points advice off.

What's it for?

Warlords and Engineers go together: every Warlord gets their Engineers. The planner shows each Warlord as a card with the full name, how full it is (e.g. 3/4) and their Engineers in small print below. If you prefer the old table, switch to "▦ Table" at the top. The number of Engineer slots per Warlord is adjustable (1–10, default 3).

How it works

  1. On the left is the queue: members without an assignment.
  2. Tap a card to open it: the Engineers then appear as full rows, with ✉ to ask, ♡ for a fixed pair and 🗑 back to the queue. "＋ Add engineer" lets you pick someone from the queue. The 🗑 in the card header sends the Warlord and all their Engineers back to the queue – the hub asks first.
  3. "▾ Expand all" opens every card at once, "▴ Collapse all" closes them again.
  4. Or drag and drop: dropping a member on a card makes them an Engineer there. At the top are the "new Warlord" and "back to queue" drop zones.
  5. Sort by name or by number of Engineers.
  6. "✉ Alliance mail" copies the finished line-up as text, ready to paste into an in-game mail. At the top it reminds engineers to give their warlord Win-Win once a day, and to learn Win-Win first.
Using the hub in a narrow window next to the game? The card view is the most comfortable then. The table uses the full window width: the bigger the window, the wider the tiles. Long names wrap onto two lines, and ✉ and ♡ only appear when you hover over a tile.

Rank shields and rank check

Each tile shows the rank as a number in a shield in the rank's colour. Many alliances assign R2 and R3 by profession, because the game doesn't show professions in the member list. That's where the R2/R3 rank check comes in: switch it on and choose whether R2 means Engineer or Warlord. Tiles are tinted by the expected profession. Anyone in the wrong spot gets a yellow border and a hint like "→ Eng.?". In the card view the name also wiggles a little; in the table a small arrow shows the direction.

✉ Ask a player

Not sure which profession someone has right now? Click ✉ on the tile: a ready-made text opens in the player's language and is copied straight away – just paste it into an in-game mail. You can still edit the text first.

  • For R2/R3 (rank check on) the text explains your rule, e.g. "Our rule: R2 = Engineer, R3 = Warlord".
  • For R1, R4 and R5 the rank says nothing about the profession, so the text asks directly: "Are you currently a Warlord or an Engineer? I have you down as: …". This text is also used when the rank check is off.
  • Under "✎ Question texts" R4/R5 can adjust both texts per language (switch "For R2/R3 | For R1, R4, R5"), with preview.

🎲 Auto-assign & fixed pairs

Lots of players waiting in the queue? "🎲 Auto-assign" does the sorting for you. Nothing is saved until you click "Apply" in the preview.

  1. New Warlords: anyone whose rank means Warlord (rank check on) becomes a Warlord.
  2. One active Engineer each: active means ★, the top third by points over the last 4 weeks. Every Warlord gets one of them where possible.
  3. The rest evenly: everyone else goes randomly to the free slots, always to the Warlord with the most free slots.
  4. Move (checkbox): if one Warlord has two active Engineers and another has none, one is handed over. And if one has two Engineers more than another, the numbers are evened out so everyone has about the same. It's only a suggestion in the preview; "Reroll" shows a different split.

Tips & limits

  • The rank check never moves anyone by itself. "Auto-assign" only suggests; you decide with "Apply".
  • Anyone who is moved has to switch Warlord in the game. Give them a quick heads-up, e.g. with ✉.
  • The automatic distribution never touches R4 and R5; assign them yourself.
  • Someone wiggles although they're in the right place? Often the rank in the hub is just out of date. Scan the member list first, then ask.
  • For R1, R4, R5 and honorary members the rank says nothing about the profession. Those tiles stay neutral.
  • Members can set their own profession in My Area.

🗂 Member management

Who sees it?R4/R5 (honorary R4 read-only). Admin area: R5 and operator.
Feature switch?No, always on

What's it for?

Keeping the member list in shape: new players, rank changes, Discord links, guests from other alliances, departures.

How it works

  1. Add member: enter name, Discord ID (optional) and rank.
  2. Login requests: accept open requests "as new member", "map to member" (link to an existing member), or reject them.
  3. Member list: tap a member to change name, Discord ID, rank and role. You also see when they last logged in.
  4. Removing: deleted players go to the archive. R5 can restore them with all their data from Admin → Archive.

Honorary roles

👁 Honorary R4

A guest from another alliance, e.g. a friendly R4. Sees everything an R4 sees, changes nothing and is never counted. Enter their home alliance and Discord ID so they can log in.

🎖 Honorary member

Stays on the list without write access. The rank check skips honorary members.

Admin area (R5)

  • Alliances: change tag and name. Renaming the tag moves all player data along. Per alliance: "accepts login requests" and the feature switches.
  • Archive: restore deleted players.
  • Transfer: move a player with all their data to another alliance.
  • Merge: combine duplicate entries (e.g. after a name change) into one player – also with an archive entry from another alliance, e.g. after a switch. Their weeks and scan values come along.
  • API tokens: see Android app.

📸 Screenshot scanner

Who sees it?R4/R5 without an honorary role. Honorary R4 and operator only see the power trend.
Feature switch?Yes (scanner)

What's it for?

Instead of typing 100 names: just scroll through the member list, power ranking, VS points (daily and weekly), kills and donations and let the scanner read them. Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely in your browser. Images are never uploaded – only the recognised values go to lwhub.

How it works

  1. Open the "Scanner" tab. It recognises fastest in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Either "Choose window & start scan", share the game window (e.g. an emulator) and scroll slowly – changed views are captured automatically. Or "Load screenshots / video".
  3. On the left is your lwhub member list, in the middle the pool of recognised tiles. Clear matches are assigned automatically.
  4. Drag unclear tiles onto the right member, or onto "New player" to add a newcomer. Anyone no longer in the alliance ends up under "Archive".
  5. Sync. The scanner then shows what was imported and where there were conflicts. lwhub sets the VS and donation ticks at the same time (see VS).

Tips & limits

  • Processing: graphics card (WebGPU) by default, otherwise CPU. You can set how many CPU cores to use.
  • At the top the scanner counts per list, e.g. "VS Tue 87 / 100". Gaps in the rank numbers (scrolled too fast) show up while you're still scrolling – just scroll back up once more.
  • If someone switches alliance, the upload finds them even with a different spelling (accents like "MonGénéral", earlier names) and takes their values along. In older weeks they still count for the alliance they were in back then.
  • You can download your captures locally for later, or discard them.
  • Players from other alliances in a list are recognised by their alliance tag and left out.
  • 🚀 Boost makes the scanner about twice as fast. While scanning it draws nothing, only the counters at the top keep going. Tiles it is sure about don't show up next to the players at all, since their values are already in the summary. At the end you only see what needs your help: unclear tiles on the right and uncertain matches with their picture next to the player. Turn Boost off to see all tiles again.
  • “⚠ unsicher” (uncertain) means the name was only read approximately, or it wasn't readable at all and the tile was matched by its unique power. Usually that's right, but a quick look is worth it. Hover over the tile to see why. If it doesn't fit, ✕ detaches it.
  • Cyrillic names (e.g. “Владимир1”) can't be read directly by the text recognition. The scanner compares them using similar-looking Latin letters. Such matches always count as uncertain.
  • At the top you see who last sent data to lwhub, e.g. “🕓 Zuletzt: Lion Tooth · 08.11.26, 20:02”. Hover over it to see the last five uploads.

📈 Power trend

Who sees it?R4/R5, honorary R4, operator
Feature switch?Part of scanner

What's it for?

How is your alliance's power developing? Active players grow during the week and put their troops to work in the big Saturday event. The trend makes that rhythm visible from your power scans, so leadership can see who's really taking off and who might appreciate a tip or a helping hand.

Verdicts
🔥 sacrifices on Saturday📈 growing➖ normal⚠️ stagnant❔ not enough data

How it works

  1. Scan the power ranking regularly. Best: Friday (before the event), Sunday (after) and once mid-week.
  2. Open Reports → Power trend, filter or sort by verdict. Former members are greyed out with their values from their time in the alliance.
  3. If needed, "Adjust thresholds", e.g. what percentage of Saturday loss counts.

🔍 Event analysis (before/after)

Who sees it?R4/R5, honorary R4 and operator (view only)
Feature switch?Part of scanner

What's it for?

"Who showed up for the event?" – answered from two scans, one before and one after. Anyone who got kills or lost power was there.

How it works

  1. Scan power and kills once before and once after the event.
  2. Open Reports → Server events (or Events → Special via 🔍). Use ‹ › to step through the saved special events; the latest one opens by itself. You can adjust the grace period in minutes, "kills from" and "loss from (%)".
  3. Check the result, use "📋 Copy names" for chat, or set the ticks in the Events tab with "Import from scanner".
  4. "✉ Copy alliance mail" puts a ready-made mail on the clipboard: event name, time and the ranking of all participants with place and kill points. Paste it in the game as an alliance mail and send it.

📱 Android app & API tokens

Who sees it?Managing tokens: R5 and operator (Admin → API tokens)
StatusThe app is in testing

What's it for?

An Android app reads rankings right on your phone (e.g. power, VS, donations) and uploads the values to lwhub. So that tools like the app can write without a Discord login, there are API tokens.

How it works

  1. Admin → API tokens → choose the alliance → "+ Create token".
  2. Give it a name (what is it for?) and pick a role: Scanner (values for known players only), R4 or R5 (same as in the web scanner). Expiry is optional; empty means no expiry.
  3. Copy the token right away. It's only shown once.
  4. Not needed any more? "Revoke token". The list shows when each token was last used.

🔒 Privacy

This is a factual overview of what lwhub stores, based on its code. It is not legal advice and not a privacy policy.

On the server

  • Discord: your Discord ID, name and avatar (cached). Only the identify scope is requested.
  • Player: player name and previous names, alliance, rank and rank changes, role, profession and preferred language.
  • Weekly data: event ticks, positive and penalty points per calendar week.
  • Teams: team types, power and hero power, if you enter them.
  • Scan values: recognised power, VS points, kills and donations with a timestamp. The screenshots themselves are not uploaded.
  • Train: schedule, queue, no-shows.
  • Presence: while lwhub is open, "last seen" is saved about every 5 minutes. R4/R5 can see the last login.
  • Login requests: Discord name, avatar, chosen alliance, player name and your note.
  • Archive: deleted players are archived with their data so R5 can restore them.
  • Security: checked or rejected API calls are logged with time, path, Discord ID and IP address. Discord logins are cached for 15 minutes, as a hash only. API tokens are stored as a hash only.

In your browser

Your Discord login, active character, language and last opened page are kept in your browser's local storage. "Log out" removes the login and character.

Who can see the data?

Each alliance only sees its own members. Within the alliance, the roles from Who sees what? apply. Special event schedules are shared between alliances with the same server number.
